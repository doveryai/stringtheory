Approximate Formulas

For quick calculations between table values:

84–95 in²: kg ≈ 0.625 × DT − 0.9
95–105 in²: kg ≈ 0.65 × DT + 0.3
106–115 in²: kg ≈ 0.69 × DT + 1.3

To convert kg to lbs: lbs = kg × 2.205

About DT Measurements

DT (Dynamic Tension) is a technical index from the ERT300 stringbed stiffness tester. The conversion to kg/lbs is an approximation — not an exact back-calculation of pulling weight.

The measured DT value is influenced by:

  • Head size and frame shape
  • Frame material and stiffness
  • String type (poly, gut, multi, hybrid)
  • String pattern density
  • Stringing machine and technique

Best practice: Measure before stringing, immediately after, and again after 24 hours to track tension loss.

Data source: ERT300.com
